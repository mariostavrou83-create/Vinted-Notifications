Your offer rules

For a £23 item: £21.50 with 50p rounding, or £21.85 to the penny. The current item price is checked before sending.

Last 3 months uses a rolling calendar-month cutoff. Pausing and resuming keeps your history and sent offers. Changing this choice starts a new scan. Buyers already offered this item will not be contacted again.

Steady, one at a time.

Native price offers, starting 5 minutes after each like, with at least three minutes between sends. New likes have priority while older favourites are processed in the gaps. No extra message is added.

  • One offer per buyer for each item.
  • Sold, reserved and hidden items are skipped.
  • Existing offers and negotiations are skipped.
  • Vinted account or rate-limit errors pause the queue.

Only favourites visible in your Vinted notifications can be processed. A large history takes longer to work through.
