New opportunities only

Existing brands and saved search types are excluded. Modern clothing and unfamiliar brands are included; there is no Y2K or vintage requirement.

What makes a strong search?

Repeated cheap items selling within 24 hours, similar items selling quickly at higher prices, and enough fresh cheap stock to buy regularly. Removed and reserved items are kept separate from sold evidence.

A marked-sold listing exposes its last visible asking price, not the final accepted offer. Recommendations remain estimates until supported by your completed trades.
