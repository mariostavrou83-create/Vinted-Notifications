Connect an existing Vinted login

Already signed in with Google, Apple or email? You can link that Vinted session here. Being signed in on your phone keeps listing links signed in, but does not connect this bot.

This setup needs desktop Chrome. iPhone Safari does not expose the required session values directly. This option verifies an existing session; it cannot remove a Vinted security check.

Where to find the two session values
  1. In desktop Chrome, sign in to your own account at Vinted UK using your usual method. Complete any checks on Vinted itself.
  2. Open Chrome's menu → More tools → Developer tools. Select Application → Storage → Cookies, then the Vinted domain.
  3. Copy only the value of access_token_web into the first box and refresh_token_web into the second. Do not copy a full cookie file.

These values give access to your Vinted account. Enter them only on this private dashboard, never in Telegram, ChatGPT or a screenshot. No browser security-check cookies are needed.

Linking lets your private bot verify this account and store its session encrypted. Both boxes clear after submission. Autobuy stays off until you enable it with spending limits.

Connect with a Vinted password

Checks the sign-in connection once without sending your email or password. This does not sign in or buy anything.

These sign-in details are sent to Vinted by your private bot. Your password is never saved. The returned account session is encrypted. Use Vinted email/password sign-in; a Google or Apple sign-in cannot be completed in this form.

If you normally use Google, use Vinted's “Forgotten your password?” option with the same email to create a Vinted password. Never enter your Google password here.

Your spending limit is set on each search. Open a search and set “Maximum total including fees & postage”. Different searches can have different budgets. A search without a budget cannot use Autobuy. Edit your searches →

The actual checkout total must fit that search’s current budget, including the item, delivery and Vinted fees. The item price must also be no higher than the price in your alert. Paused, archived or deleted searches cannot buy.

Set your address, delivery choice and payment method in Vinted first. A bank may still require confirmation. If the final total or delivery choice cannot be verified, the bot stops before paying. An uncertain payment is never automatically retried.
